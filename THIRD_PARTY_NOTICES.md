## Australian Food Composition Database

The bundled generic-food catalogue is a curated subset based on the
**Australian Food Composition Database**, published by Food Standards
Australia New Zealand (FSANZ).

- Source: <https://www.foodstandards.gov.au/science-data/food-nutrient-databases/afcd>
- Licence: [Creative Commons Attribution 2.5 Australia](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.5/au/)
- Attribution: Australian Food Composition Database, Food Standards Australia
  New Zealand.

Values are rounded for tracking and serving sizes are application defaults.
They are not a substitute for the current package label or professional
dietary advice.

## Australian restaurant nutrition

Selected KFC Australia and McDonald's Australia menu items use rounded
nutrition values and typical serving weights published in their consumer
nutrition information.

- KFC Australia nutrition: <https://www.kfc.com.au/nutrition>
- McDonald's Australia nutrition:
  <https://mcdonalds.com.au/help-centre/article/kA02s000000XbHCCA0>

Restaurant recipes, serving weights and menus change. Treat bundled values as
estimates and check the restaurant's current nutrition guide when precision
matters. Product and restaurant names are trademarks of their respective
owners; Feizai Tracker is not affiliated with or endorsed by either restaurant.

selected by a user are cached in that user's local data file.
